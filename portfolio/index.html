About me

Who I am

I am a Business and Engineering student who builds AI agents and automated workflows on top of existing business systems.

My work combines process analysis with ERP and BI experience and LLM tooling such as Claude, Claude Code and n8n. I like to take a manual, repetitive process, understand it properly, and turn it into something that runs by itself and is documented well enough for a team to trust.

I have delivered projects end to end: a publicly deployed 3D printing kiosk that connects an ERP, a touchscreen frontend and a printer fleet, ERP and IoT automation on Azure and Odoo, and BI solutions in Power BI.

GoalDrive AI enabled business development and process automation in German industry.
